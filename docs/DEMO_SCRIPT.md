# Demo video script (3 minutes)

Film on a phone with screen recording. Use a repair you have the right to publish, in 1080p, with
each screw shown. Everything below must be real footage of the product: no mock-ups, no cut that
hides a wait without saying so.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Hands stuck halfway through a repair, then the finished guide | "Most instructions are written by someone who already knows how. FixScribe turns one phone video of a repair into a guide that shows where each claim comes from." |
| 0:15-0:35 | Upload the video, then the progress page revealing what it finds | "It works out the object, the job and the hazards. Gas, mains panels and airbags are refused by fixed rules before any step is written." Say the real waiting time on screen. |
| 0:35-1:10 | The guide: photo with the part cut out, numbered screws, quote, proof dots, flip to the proof | "Muse Spark points at the part and SAM 3.1 cuts it out. Each step keeps the second of video and the words it rests on. What cannot be proven is marked, not hidden." Open one unproven claim on purpose. |
| 1:10-1:30 | The way back and the fastener count | "From taking it apart, it writes the way back and checks that every screw that came out goes back in." |
| 1:30-2:25 | Live coach with a real camera on the object: step change, marks appear dashed then solid, say "next" and ask a question | "Point the camera at the job. It says which step you are on, marks the parts, and you control it by voice, hands free." Keep the real delay in the shot: an answer takes about 8 s, the marks longer. |
| 2:25-2:45 | The README's evaluation tables | "We measured it against answer keys. On two videos it follows the step 88 to 95 % of the time within 8 seconds. Some numbers are lower, and we publish those too." |
| 2:45-3:00 | Model list and the guide | "Every model has one job: Spark, SAM 3.1 and Muse Voice run inside the product through the Meta Model API." Add Muse Wonder only if the agent ran in this demo. |

## What to film by hand

The live coach, on the deployed address, about 40 to 60 seconds in one take: the camera on the object,
a step change, marks going from dashed to solid, "next" said aloud and one spoken question. The rest
can be assembled from captures of the real pages.

## Before filming

- Run the upload and the coach once that same day, on the deployed address, from a phone.
- Replace the numbers above if the video in the demo measures differently.
- Keep faces, documents and other people's footage out of the frame.
