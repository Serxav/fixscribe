Instructions people trust

Most instructions are written by someone who already knows how. They skip the step that seems obvious, and that is the step where you get stuck.

The person who just did the job knows it best. FixScribe keeps their hands, their words and their order of work, and checks each claim against the video before it reaches the guide.

If it cannot prove a step, it says so. If it is not sure, it asks.

IHow it works

One video in.
A proven guide out.

  1. 01

    It finds out what it is looking at

    The object, the task and its hazards. Fixed safety rules run first: gas, mains panels and airbags get no do-it-yourself steps.

  2. 02

    It watches and listens

    The whole video, image and sound together, with the narration transcribed and timed to the second.

  3. 03

    An agent investigates

    It names every part like a furniture manual — A, B, C — looks at the frames that matter and points at each part with the context of what is being built.

  4. 04

    Every claim is checked outside the model

    A part counts only when SAM 3.1 cuts it out where the agent pointed. A step counts only when its parts are proven, its times fit and the words quoted were really said.

  5. 05

    When it is unsure, it asks you

    One short question on the exact frame. You approve the guide before anyone else sees it.

  6. 06

    Forward and back

    The guide to take it apart and the guide to put it back together, with the parts that go where.
