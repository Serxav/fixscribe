How it works

One video in. Five things out.

  1. 1

    It finds out what it is

    Muse Spark looks at a few frames and the first spoken words: a phone, a washing machine, a chair, a car. The object, the task and its hazards decide everything that follows.

  2. 2

    It watches and listens

    Spark watches the whole video and Muse Voice transcribes the narration. Step cuts come from your words ("step", "paso"), the pauses of your hands and scene changes — then the unclear steps are checked again on a timecoded clip.

  3. 3

    SAM 3.1 follows every part

    SAM tracks the part you remove and every fastener through each step clip. Screws that come out are numbered; the part is cut out along its real shape for the 3D views.

  4. 4

    It proves and protects

    Every sentence of the guide shows its source: the second of video, your words, the SAM measurement. Fixed safety rules run first — gas, mains panels and airbags get no DIY steps.

  5. 5

    It makes the film

    Spark writes the voice-over in your language. The film plays the SAM clips with captions and voice to take it apart — and backwards, step by step, to put it back together.
