Instructions people trust

Most instructions are written by someone who already knows how. They skip the step that seems obvious, and that is the step where you get stuck.

The person who just did the job knows it best. FixScribe keeps their hands, their words and their order of work, and checks each claim against the video before it reaches the guide.

If it cannot prove a step, it says so. If it is not sure, it asks.
