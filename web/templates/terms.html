Terms

Using FixScribe safely

FixScribe writes a guide from your video with Meta's AI models. Read this before you upload.

A guide can be wrong

Every step is checked against the video, but the guide is written by AI and can still miss or misread something. Check each step against what you see, follow the maker's instructions, unplug the power and wear the right protection. You are responsible for the repair you do.

Jobs FixScribe will not guide

Gas appliances, mains electrical panels, airbags, refrigerant circuits, high-voltage batteries and the inside of microwave ovens need a professional. FixScribe recognizes them and gives no steps.

Your video

  • Upload only videos you made or have the right to share.
  • The video stays yours. You let FixScribe and Meta's models process it to make your guide, and nothing else.
  • You can delete it at any time: see Privacy.

Limits

One video of up to 10 minutes, in MP4 or MOV. The service takes a limited number of videos per day and may be unavailable at times.

No warranty

FixScribe is offered as it is, without any warranty. As far as the law allows, its makers are not liable for damage caused by a repair done with one of its guides.
