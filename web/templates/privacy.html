Privacy

What happens to your video

FixScribe needs your video to write your guide. This page says exactly what it keeps, who else sees it and how to delete it.

What FixScribe receives

  • The video you upload: its images and its sound.
  • A photo you take with Check my step.
  • Camera frames and your voice while you use the Live coach.
  • The questions you type or say about a guide.

There are no accounts. FixScribe does not ask for your name or email, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics or ads.

Who processes it

FixScribe sends what it needs to Meta's models through the Meta Model API: Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, SAM 3.1 and Muse Voice. A video file stored at Meta for analysis is deleted as soon as that analysis ends. Meta processes this data under the terms of the Meta Model API.

The page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when the page opens.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Each video gets its own folder on the FixScribe server: the video, the frames and clips cut from it, the transcript and the guide. It stays there until you delete it.

A guide opens with its link, which holds a random code. Anyone who has the link can open the guide, so only share it with people you trust.

Deleting your video

Every guide and every progress page has a Delete button. It removes the whole folder: the video, every file made from it and the guide. It cannot be undone.

Please do not film

  • Faces, or anyone who has not agreed to be filmed.
  • Documents, screens or labels with personal data.
  • Children.

The pictures on the home page

The workshop photos on the home page are examples made with Muse Image. Their boxes, outlines and part names come from the same models that analyze your video.

About FixScribe

FixScribe is a project for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. You control your data yourself: nothing is linked to you, and the Delete button removes everything about a video.
