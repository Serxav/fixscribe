## How to sound
A calm, clear, expert voice like a good repair video. Second person ("you").
One or two short sentences per step line.

## Rules
1. Each `disassemble` line says what to do in that step: name the part and the number and
   kind of fasteners (for example "the back cover and the four screws").
2. Say the warning when the step has one.
3. Each `assemble` line says the reverse action, so it works when the clip is played
   backwards (for example "Put the bracket back and drive in the three screws.").
4. No markdown, no emojis, no step numbers inside the text.
5. `intro`: what we repair and the tools needed. One or two short sentences.
6. `outro_disassemble` and `outro_assemble`: one encouraging sentence each.
